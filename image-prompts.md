# Image prompts — "Porto-7" story

For OpenAI image generation. Landscape 1536×1024 unless noted.

**How to keep the characters consistent:** generate 1 (Kevin) and 2 (cast) first. For every later prompt, attach those
two images as references and start with "Use the attached character sheets." Paste the STYLE block in front of every
prompt. Jokes and quotes go on the slides, not into the images — the only text in an image is where a prompt asks for it.

## STYLE (paste before every prompt)

```
Style: flat vector cartoon, retro 1970s sci-fi comic look, bold clean outlines, limited palette of deep navy, teal,
warm orange and cream, soft grain texture, expressive funny faces, simple backgrounds, lots of empty space for slide
text. Original characters only, not based on any existing film or franchise. No text unless asked for.
```

## 1. Kevin — character sheet

```
Character sheet of Kevin, a young junior on-call engineer on a space station. Skinny, messy hair, dark circles under
his eyes, oversized teal station jumpsuit with too many pockets, a pager clipped to the collar, holding a huge coffee
mug. Hopeful but always slightly panicked. Show him front view, side view, and five facial expressions: panic, hope,
exhaustion, tears of joy, triumphant. Plain cream background.
```

## 2. Cast — lineup

```
Lineup character sheet of five original sci-fi parody characters on a plain cream background:
1) Lord Manager — a very tall corporate boss in glossy black armor with a long cape, a breathing mask shaped like a
   necktie, holding a tablet with a falling chart.
2) Master O'Tel — a tiny, very old, wrinkled green-skinned sage with huge ears, a walking stick, sitting on a pile of
   glowing horizontal timeline bars.
3) TR-4CE — a tall, thin, golden humanoid protocol robot, nervous posture, hands raised.
4) B3-EP — a small round white-and-orange droid on three wheels with a dome head and a tiny screen showing "400".
5) Scrappy — a stocky engineer with a big ginger moustache, oily overalls and a giant wrench.
Label each character with its name under it.
```

## 3. Prologue — station Porto-7

```
Wide shot of a cosy, slightly shabby orbital trading station named "PORTO-7" (the name painted on its hull) floating
above a planet, small cargo shuttles flying to and from it. In the far corner a tiny desert colony planet where one
colonist is angrily shaking a smoking order terminal. Lonely, funny mood.
```

## 4. Prologue — "no errors here"

```
Kevin sits at a big control console showing only green checkmarks, smiling and relaxed with his coffee. Behind him,
a giant hologram of Lord Manager leans over him menacingly. Through the window, far away on a planet, a colonist's
terminal is on fire. Kevin has not noticed anything.
```

## 5. Theory — the swamp lesson

```
A misty swamp planet. Tiny Master O'Tel stands on a stump and teaches Kevin, who sits in the mud with a notebook.
Between them float glowing horizontal bars of different lengths, nested like a waterfall timeline (a parent bar with
shorter child bars under it), forming the path of one voyage. A pile of crumpled paper logs is burning in a small fire
next to Kevin.
```

TR-4CE, the golden protocol robot, solemnly hands Kevin an ancient glowing orange "black box" cube with the letters
"OTLP" engraved on it. In the background, many different starship fleets (different ship shapes) all hold the same
cube. Ceremonial, slightly absurd mood.
```

Split scene. Left: the hull of a shuttle covered with simple sensors that show only a clock and a thumbs-up, looking
dumb and smug. Right: a shuttle captain writing detailed notes into a glowing logbook while Kevin points at it
approvingly. B3-EP rolls by beeping, its screen showing "400", with a big question mark above it.
```

```

the crates at once stands unused next to it. Kevin watches, facepalming.

## 11. Regression — new firmware

```
Engine room of a starship. Scrappy the ginger-moustached engineer hugs a groaning, smoking engine, a big shiny sticker

A rickety asteroid mining outpost with a delivery window. Out of three shuttles waiting in line, one is getting
