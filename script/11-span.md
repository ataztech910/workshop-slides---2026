# 11 · Logs you have. Story you have not.

`step: span` · Part 1 · Theory · **0:16:30 → 0:20** (3,5 мин)

**На экране:** картинка 05, Master O'Tel и Kevin.

## Текст

**[0:16:30]** Kevin goes to Master O'Tel. He says: "Master, I have logs. Lots of logs."

And Master O'Tel says: *"Logs you have. Story you have not."*

*(пауза)*

And then: *"Grep leads to anger. Anger leads to more grep."*

**[0:17:30]** Here is what he means. On a station, every crew member keeps their own diary. The pilot writes "took off at nine". The cargo officer writes "loaded twelve crates". The docking officer writes "something docked at eleven". Those are logs. Each one is true. But nobody wrote down that these three lines are *the same voyage*.

To get the story, you grep three diaries and you guess which lines belong together. At 3 am. With Lord Manager behind you.

**[0:18:30]** A **span** is one leg of a voyage. It has a name — what happened. A start and an end — how long it took. A status — did it go well. And **attributes** — the facts: which cargo, which colony, how heavy.

A **trace** is the log of one whole voyage. All its legs, together, connected.

So the difference is not "more logs". The difference is: a trace knows which pieces belong to the same request. That is the story.
