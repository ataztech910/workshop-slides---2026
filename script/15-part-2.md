**[0:30]** Part two. OpenTelemetry by hand. Small pieces, your keyboard.

Eight missions. You build your own receiver, you send your first span, you make it a tree. Then the shop starts sending its own spans, you add a span of your own, and at the end you ask your first AI agent — over the same raw data.

Every mission has a number, and one command: `npm run mission` and the number. It tells you what to do, and it ticks off what is done. When it prints a star — the mission is complete.
